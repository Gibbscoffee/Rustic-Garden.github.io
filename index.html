About

One name, five ways the land is put to work

The Rustic Garden is a family-scale farm and garden in Kenya, set out on roughly half a hectare of garden and a little over six-tenths of a hectare of the wider shamba. Everything grown, raised and hosted here sits under the single name, so visitors, customers and partners always know whose land they are on.

The same ground that hosts a gathering also grows coffee and macadamia, holds fish ponds, and serves as a place to try out better ways of farming.

  • 5lines of work under one name
  • 0.51 hagarden area (5,107 m²)
  • 0.60 hashamba boundary (6,030 m²)
